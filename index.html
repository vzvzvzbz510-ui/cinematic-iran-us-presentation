The Nuclear Issue

Nuclear energy can produce electricity, but countries fear that some programmes may be used to make weapons. Iran says its work is peaceful, while the United States and other nations worry about the risk.

Economic Sanctions

Sanctions are penalties that limit trade, banking, and investment. They can pressure a government, but they also hurt ordinary people by raising prices, reducing jobs, and making daily life more difficult.
